# Food safety and operating checklist

FoodBridge is a coordination tool, not a food-safety certification service.

Before approving a donation, an administrator should confirm:
- Food identity, vegetarian/non-vegetarian classification and known allergens.
- Preparation time and a realistic safe-consumption deadline.
- Safe handling, packaging, storage temperature and transport duration.
- Receiving organization's capacity, dietary needs and acceptance.
- A responsible contact at pickup and handover.

Reject food with unknown preparation/storage history, damaged packaging, contamination, spoilage, or a deadline that cannot be met. Do not treat a donor-entered deadline as proof that food is safe. Establish written operating procedures and obtain appropriate local regulatory guidance before launch.
